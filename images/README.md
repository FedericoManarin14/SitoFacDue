| `collanti-card.jpg` | Pails/buckets of glue stacked on a wooden pallet (landscape) |
| `abrasivi-card.jpg` | Industrial boxes/products on a red metal pallet (landscape) |
| `vinilica1.jpg` | Vinylic glue product photo #1 (e.g. bucket or container) |

| `poliuretaniche2.jpg` | Polyurethane glue product photo #2 |

## Notes
- Recommended formats: JPG for photos, PNG for logo (with transparency)
- Product images for collanti should have white or neutral backgrounds
- Hero image should be high resolution (min 1920×1080px) for full-viewport display
- `abrasivi-product.jpg` is used as a placeholder for all abrasive product cards until individual product images are available
